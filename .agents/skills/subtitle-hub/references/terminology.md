# Series terminology

## SH-TRANS-007 — One series guide

Each series directory maintains one active `series-guide.md` containing stable `term_id`, category, source-language form, canonical Simplified Chinese, prohibited/candidate variants, scope, evidence source, status, and notes. Do not duplicate a glossary per work.

Sources may include official Chinese releases, source-language official material, reputable reference pages, actual source subtitles/audio, and prior confirmed repository decisions. Public wikis discover candidates but do not decide conflicts alone.

Statuses are `confirmed`, `provisional`, and `pending-user-confirmation`. Confirmed entries apply across their declared series scope. Do not resolve substantive alternatives by frequency. Present candidates, original term, evidence, and impact to the user; record the result.

A work-specific deviation requires a `project-guide.md` override citing `term_id`, exact scope, work evidence, and user confirmation. Automated terminology checks produce candidates; they do not bulk-replace names or context-sensitive terms.

Before proofreading a new installment, read the series guide, extract new source-language proper-name candidates, align confirmed terms, research only unresolved additions/conflicts, request decisions where needed, update the same guide, and complete a difference audit.
