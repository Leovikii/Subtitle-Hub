# Simplified Chinese text standard

## SH-ZH-001 — Text goal

Write accurate, concise, natural Simplified Chinese readable in one viewing. Preserve character voice and plot information. Avoid artificial writtenese and time-bound internet slang unless the work itself calls for it.

## SH-ZH-002 — Built-in minimal punctuation

This repository uses an internal baseline; do not re-query external guides during routine work.

- Omit commas and full stops in ordinary dialogue. Use one ASCII space for a natural in-sentence pause; omit a sentence-final full stop.
- Avoid enumeration commas and semicolons unless omission creates ambiguity; `、` is allowed for a genuinely ambiguous list.
- Use full-width `？` for questions and `！` only for necessary strong force. Do not use `！？`, `？！`, `？？`, or `！！`.
- Use `……` (two U+2026) for hesitation, trailing meaning, or fade-out; not `...` or U+22EF.
- Use `——` for interruption/abrupt turn; not two ASCII hyphens.
- Use `：` only for a clear label, quotation, or explanation relationship.
- Use Simplified Chinese quotation marks `“”`, nested `‘’`, without extra spaces.

Formal documents, code, terminals, signs, or deliberately reproduced on-screen text may keep source punctuation when recorded as a project exception.

## SH-ZH-003 — Spacing, characters, numbers

- Put one ASCII space at a semantic boundary between Chinese and Latin/Greek letters or Arabic numerals; do not split a model/code such as `EX178`, `BRAVO-1`, or `α-1`.
- Do not add spaces around Chinese punctuation or padding spaces at line edges.
- Avoid full-width Latin letters/numerals unless reproducing screen text.
- Prefer Chinese numerals for ordinary small approximations when space allows; use ASCII numerals for exact time, measurement, coordinates, models, dates, phone numbers, and similar data.
- Do not mix numeral systems within one number or silently convert currency. Prefer audience-readable metric units unless the original unit is plot-relevant.

## SH-ZH-004 — Names and terminology

Use confirmed official/series forms. Use `·` where a foreign personal name needs separation. Keep ships, equipment, organizations, ranks, and technical terms consistent across episodes. Register new uncertainty instead of inventing a formal translation.

## SH-ZH-005 — Language quality and notes

Check grammatical roles, modifier scope, negation, rhetorical questions, conditionals, causality, reference continuity, command/register hierarchy, collocation, ambiguity, and offensive-force equivalence. Do not sanitize or intensify without evidence.

Subtitles are not encyclopedia notes. A short note is allowed only when natural translation cannot prevent a real comprehension loss. It must be plot-relevant, brief, non-editorial, non-obstructive, and defined in the project guide with style/position/width.
